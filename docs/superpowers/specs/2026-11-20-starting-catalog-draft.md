## 1. The Ledger
- **Series:** An Adebayo Ledger Novel, Book One (Book Two, *The River's Due*, is announced in the book)
- **Author / voice:** Ren Hawthorne
- **Type:** fiction (contemporary mythic fantasy)
- **Amazon:** https://a.co/d/0aPWMork (short link; still to confirm which link is which book)
- **Cover:** `assets/covers/the_ledger.jpg`. Palette: indigo night, amber crossroads glow.

**Draft blurb (spoiler-free, from the prologue and chapters 1–2)**
> Yemi Adebayo spent thirty years as a bank underwriter. Every number checked twice. Every
> promise in writing. Nothing owed that wasn't itemized. Two years after her mother's death,
> the one account she still hasn't closed is the locked shrine room at the end of the hall.
> Behind the door she finds her mother's ledger: dates, names, and debts paid in something
> other than money. The oldest entry was written the night Yemi was born. Her mother's
> bargain with Èṣù still reads *Terms: pending*.

**Tropes / hooks:** inherited ledger of debts to the gods · Yoruba orisha in modern Atlanta ·
a heroine over 50 starting over · mother–daughter–granddaughter · a skeptic forced to
believe · bargains with a price nobody wrote down · first in a series

3. "Carefulness, it turned out, was not the same thing as tending."
4. "Not superstition. Bookkeeping."
5. "Decoration was for people trying to hide what they actually wanted."
6. "She wrote *pending*."
7. "Ledger. F. Adebayo. Entry 0010. Debtor: F. Adebayo. Counterparty: Èṣù. Terms: pending."

**Do not say / spoil (proposed):** how the Èṣù debt is resolved; anything from the later

## 2. The Ones Nobody Came For
- **Series:** A Keeper Novel (Book Two is announced in the book)
- **Author / voice:** Ren Hawthorne
- **Type:** fiction (quiet / cozy fantasy)
- **Amazon:** https://a.co/d/0dXjLJ2d (short link; still to confirm which link is which book)
- **Cover:** `assets/covers/the_ones_nobody_came_for.jpg`. Palette: dusk blue, warm barn-light amber.

**Draft blurb (spoiler-free, from chapters 1–2)**
> Sixteen-year-old Wren has been through five foster placements. She has learned to fit her
> life into two bags and never be the one who goes back to the car. Her sixth placement is
> a run-down fairgrounds in Nickel Falls, Ohio. There a woman named Roz runs a sanctuary for
> creatures nobody else would take: a grey giant who won't sleep, a blind resident who walks
> only his route, retired message birds the fax machine made obsolete, and a hound working a
> fence line for something no one can name. Wren has a secret. When she touches an animal,
> she knows what it feels. She has never told anyone. At the Fairgrounds, for the first time,
> it might be useful.

**Tropes / hooks:** found family · magical creature sanctuary · foster kid finds a place ·
gruff caretaker with rules · animal empathy · small-town Ohio · quiet fantasy · first in a series

**Quotable lines (verbatim)**
